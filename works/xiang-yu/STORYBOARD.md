# Xiang Yu study: shared storyboard

The two implementations share the same 627-frame, 30 fps timeline and the same
visual assets. Times below are relative to the local excerpt, not the whole
recording. Captions are supplied privately from an LRC file.

| Approximate time | Visual beat |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 s | The warrior and the woman face one another in the riverside camp. |
| 5–10 s | A closer view of their farewell and the ribbon between their hands. |
| 10–16 s | The tent dissolves into the riverbank; the warrior turns away. |
| 16–20.9 s | The distance grows as the image darkens. |

The restrained camera movement, pose dissolves and embers are intentional.
This is a framework and art-direction study; the still-based character motion
is not a substitute for fully animated acting in a finished long-form MV.
