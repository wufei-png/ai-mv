# Xiang Yu — pixel narrative study

This first study compares Remotion and HyperFrames using the same timed excerpt,
character atlas, background, storyboard and output profile. It is a local
prototype, not a published music video or an assessment of publication rights.

The visual direction is restrained historical pixel art: a warrior and his
partner share a quiet farewell in a riverside camp. The study uses cinematic
framing and light with simplified, painterly surroundings. The timeline is
approximately 01:10–01:31 of the supplied recording; final cue positions remain
subject to listening review.

Source audio and lyric files are user-supplied and are not stored in this
repository. The project contains no copy of the recording or original lyrics.
No public viewing link has been approved for this study.
